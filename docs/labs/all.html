Lab 1. Your first flight

Level 1: Getting started
  • You cannot break anything. This is a simulator. Crashing costs nothing.
  • If anything goes wrong, press R. The drone returns to the start, good as new.
  • There is no time limit. Only your best try counts.
Show the keys
W / S   up / down            Arrow Up / Down     forward / back
A / D   turn left / right    Arrow Left / Right  slide left / right
T  take off    L  land    R  start over    C  change view    P  flight plan
1  Position hold    2  Altitude hold    3  Stabilized
Space  motors on / off       Shift  full stick

Orange arms mark the front of the drone. Pressing 1 at any time gives you all the help back.

Goal: take off, move around, and land.

Start

  1. Click Open the trainer at the top of this page. Use Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.
  2. Click once on the picture of the field.
  3. Check that "Position hold" is selected at the top right.

Do

  1. Press T. The drone takes off by itself and waits.
  2. Tap Arrow Up. The drone moves away. Let go. It stops by itself.
  3. Tap Arrow Left, then Arrow Right.
  4. Tap W to go higher. Tap S to come lower.
  5. Fly back over the orange ring.
  6. Press L. The drone lands by itself.

You are done when you have taken off and landed three times.

If you get lost: press R and begin again.

Think about it: what did the drone do when you let go of the keys?

Goal: hold the drone inside a box for 15 seconds.

Start

  1. Click Open the trainer at the top of this page.
  2. Click once on the picture of the field.
  3. Check that "Position hold" is selected at the top right.
  4. Under "Drills", click Hover box. An orange box appears above the ring.

Do

  1. Press T to take off.
  2. Tap W once or twice so the drone sits in the middle of the box.
  3. The box turns green when you are inside. The counter at the top runs up to 15 s.
  4. Make small corrections with the arrow keys. Small taps work best.
  5. When the counter reaches 15, your score appears.
  6. Press R to try again for a better score.

You are done when you score 70 or more.

If the box stays orange: you are too high, too low, or the nose has turned. Press R.

Think about it: did big key presses or small taps work better?

Goal: feel the difference between the three flight modes.

Start

  1. Click Open the trainer at the top of this page.
  2. Click once on the picture of the field.
  3. Check that "Landing assist" is ticked in the Sensors section. It is ticked by default.

Do

  1. Press 1 for Position hold. Press T. Tap Arrow Up, then let go. The drone stops.
  2. Press 2 for Altitude hold. Tap Arrow Up, then let go. The drone keeps drifting.
  3. Tap Arrow Down to stop the drift. You are now the brakes.
  4. Press 3 for Stabilized. Now you also control the height. Tap W and S gently to stay level.
  5. Fly for one minute in Stabilized. Try to stay near the orange ring.
  6. Press 1 at any time to get all the help back. Press L to land.

You are done when you have flown one minute in Stabilized mode.

If it gets away from you: press 1. The drone stops and holds. Nothing bad happens.

Orange arms mark the front of the drone. Pressing 1 at any time gives you all the help back.

Goal: bring in a real field, choose where you stand, and fly a survey over it.

You need: a field boundary saved from Google Earth. To make one: open Google Earth, draw a polygon around a field, right-click it, choose "Save Place As", and save it as KML.

Start

  1. Click Open the trainer at the top of this page.
  2. Click once on the picture of the field.
  3. Press P. The flight plan window opens.

Do

  1. Under "Import your field", click Choose Files and pick your KML file.
  2. Your boundary appears on the map. Flight lines fill it.
  3. Click Set home, then click the spot on the map where you would stand. The H moves there.
  4. Set "Altitude above ground" to 40 and "Speed" to 5.
  5. Read the results table. Check the flight time against your battery.
  6. Set the time selector to 8x time. Click Fly this plan.
  7. When the drone has landed, click Export KML.
  8. Save the text as a .kml file and open it in Google Earth. Your flight lines sit on your real field.

You are done when you see your flight lines in Google Earth.

If "Set home" is grey or refuses: no field is loaded yet. Do step 1 first.

Think about it: where would you stand to keep the drone in sight for the whole flight?
