# Lab 6. Flying with less help

Level 1: Getting started

Trainer address: https://alibulentkoc.github.io/multirotor-flight-trainer/

- You cannot break anything. This is a simulator. Crashing costs nothing.
- If anything goes wrong, press **R**. The drone returns to the start, good as new.
- There is no time limit. Only your best try counts.

**The keys**

```
W / S   up / down            Arrow Up / Down     forward / back
A / D   turn left / right    Arrow Left / Right  slide left / right
T  take off    L  land    R  start over    C  change view    P  flight plan
1  Position hold    2  Altitude hold    3  Stabilized
Space  motors on / off       Shift  full stick
```

Orange arms mark the front of the drone. Pressing **1** at any time gives you all the help back.

---

**Goal:** feel the difference between the three flight modes.

**Start**
1. Open the trainer address.
2. Click once on the picture of the field.
3. Check that "Landing assist" is ticked in the Sensors section. It is ticked by default.

**Do**
1. Press **1** for Position hold. Press **T**. Tap **Arrow Up**, then let go. The drone stops.
2. Press **2** for Altitude hold. Tap **Arrow Up**, then let go. The drone keeps drifting.
3. Tap **Arrow Down** to stop the drift. You are now the brakes.
4. Press **3** for Stabilized. Now you also control the height. Tap **W** and **S** gently to stay level.
5. Fly for one minute in Stabilized. Try to stay near the orange ring.
6. Press **1** at any time to get all the help back. Press **L** to land.

**You are done when** you have flown one minute in Stabilized mode.

**If it gets away from you:** press **1**. The drone stops and holds. Nothing bad happens.

**Think about it:** what does Position hold do for you that you had to do yourself in Stabilized?
