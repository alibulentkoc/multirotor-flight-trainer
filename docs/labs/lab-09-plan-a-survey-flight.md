# Lab 9. Plan a survey flight

Level 1: Getting started

Trainer address: https://alibulentkoc.github.io/multirotor-flight-trainer/

- You cannot break anything. This is a simulator. Crashing costs nothing.
- If anything goes wrong, press **R**. The drone returns to the start, good as new.
- There is no time limit. Only your best try counts.

**The keys**

```
W / S   up / down            Arrow Up / Down     forward / back
A / D   turn left / right    Arrow Left / Right  slide left / right
T  take off    L  land    R  start over    C  change view    P  flight plan
1  Position hold    2  Altitude hold    3  Stabilized
Space  motors on / off       Shift  full stick
```

Orange arms mark the front of the drone. Pressing **1** at any time gives you all the help back.

---

**Goal:** plan a mapping flight, see how height changes it, and let the drone fly it.

**Start**
1. Open the trainer address.
2. Click once on the picture of the field.
3. Press **P**. The flight plan window opens.

**Do**
1. Check that **Survey area** is selected at the top of the window.
2. Click **Whole field**. Orange flight lines appear on the map.
3. Read the results table. Write down the GSD, the number of lines, and the number of photos.
4. Change "Altitude above ground" from 40 to 20. Read the table again.
5. Change "Side overlap" from 70 to 50. Watch the lines move apart.
6. Set the altitude back to 40 and the side overlap back to 70.
7. Set the time selector to **8x time**. Click **Fly this plan**.
8. Watch the yellow triangle follow the lines. Green dots are photos. The drone lands by itself.

**You are done when** the drone has finished the plan and landed.

**If a red warning appears:** read it. It tells you what to change. Nothing is broken.

**Think about it:** you halved the height. What happened to the GSD and to the number of photos?
