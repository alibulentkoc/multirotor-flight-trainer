# Lab 11. You are the brakes

Level 2: Less help

Trainer address: https://alibulentkoc.github.io/multirotor-flight-trainer/

- You cannot break anything. This is a simulator. Crashing costs nothing.
- If anything goes wrong, press **R**. The drone returns to the start, good as new.
- There is no time limit. Only your best try counts.

**The keys**

```
W / S   up / down            Arrow Up / Down     forward / back
A / D   turn left / right    Arrow Left / Right  slide left / right
T  take off    L  land    R  start over    C  change view    P  flight plan
1  Position hold    2  Altitude hold    3  Stabilized
Space  motors on / off       Shift  full stick
```

Orange arms mark the front of the drone. Pressing **1** at any time gives you all the help back.

---

**Goal:** hold the drone in the box when it no longer stops by itself.

**Start**
1. Open the trainer address in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.
2. Click once on the picture of the field.
3. Under "Drills", click **Hover box**. An orange box appears above the ring.

**Do**
1. Press **T** to take off. Tap **W** once or twice to center the drone in the box.
2. Press **2** for Altitude hold. The drone still holds its height, but it now drifts.
3. When it drifts, tap the opposite arrow key to stop it. Then let go.
4. Use short taps. A long press sends it out of the box.
5. The box is green while you are inside. The counter runs to 15 s.
6. Press **R** to try again.

**You are done when** you finish the drill with a score of 50 or more.

**If it keeps sliding away:** you are pressing too long. Tap, wait, and watch before the next tap.

**Think about it:** why does the drone keep moving after you let go of the key?
