# Lab 13. Fly nose-first

Level 2: Less help

Trainer address: https://alibulentkoc.github.io/multirotor-flight-trainer/

- You cannot break anything. This is a simulator. Crashing costs nothing.
- If anything goes wrong, press **R**. The drone returns to the start, good as new.
- There is no time limit. Only your best try counts.

**The keys**

```
W / S   up / down            Arrow Up / Down     forward / back
A / D   turn left / right    Arrow Left / Right  slide left / right
T  take off    L  land    R  start over    C  change view    P  flight plan
1  Position hold    2  Altitude hold    3  Stabilized
Space  motors on / off       Shift  full stick
```

Orange arms mark the front of the drone. Pressing **1** at any time gives you all the help back.

---

**Goal:** fly a lap around the tree with the nose pointing where you are going.

**Start**
1. Open the trainer address.
2. Click once on the picture of the field.
3. Check that "Position hold" is selected at the top right.
4. Find the tree. It is ahead of you and a little to the left.

**Do**
1. Press **T** to take off. Hold **W** until "Altitude AGL" reads about 3 m.
2. Hold **Arrow Up** to fly toward the tree. Stop about 5 m before it.
3. Hold **Arrow Up** and tap **A** at the same time. The drone curves left, like a car.
4. Keep the tree on your left and go all the way around it.
5. Now go around the other way. Hold **Arrow Up** and tap **D**.
6. Fly back to the orange ring and press **L** to land.

**You are done when** you have flown one lap in each direction.

**If you cannot tell which way it points:** look for the orange arms. Or press **C** for the chase view, then **C** twice more to return.

**Good to know:** obstacle avoidance is on. If you get too close to the tree, the drone stops by itself.

**Think about it:** when the drone flies toward you, which key moves it to your right?
