# Lab 14. A real landing

Level 2: Less help

Trainer address: https://alibulentkoc.github.io/multirotor-flight-trainer/

- You cannot break anything. This is a simulator. Crashing costs nothing.
- If anything goes wrong, press **R**. The drone returns to the start, good as new.
- There is no time limit. Only your best try counts.

**The keys**

```
W / S   up / down            Arrow Up / Down     forward / back
A / D   turn left / right    Arrow Left / Right  slide left / right
T  take off    L  land    R  start over    C  change view    P  flight plan
1  Position hold    2  Altitude hold    3  Stabilized
Space  motors on / off       Shift  full stick
```

Orange arms mark the front of the drone. Pressing **1** at any time gives you all the help back.

---

**Goal:** land gently with no help from the landing assist.

**Start**
1. Open the trainer address.
2. Click once on the picture of the field.
3. In the Sensors section, **untick "Landing assist"**.
4. Under "Drills", click **Precision landing**. A round target appears ahead and to the right.

**Do**
1. Press **T** to take off. Fly to the target with the arrow keys.
2. Put the crosshair of the "Down camera" picture on the center of the target.
3. Press **3** for Stabilized. You now control the height.
4. Tap **S** to start down. Watch the "Climb" number.
5. Keep "Climb" between -0.5 and -1.0 m/s. Tap **W** to slow down if it goes past -1.0.
6. Slow to about -0.5 m/s for the last meter.
7. After you touch down, press **Space** to switch the motors off.

**You are done when** you land three times without a crash. The score does not matter.

**If you crash:** read the message. It tells you how fast you hit. Press **R** and come down slower.

**Think about it:** why is a slow, steady descent safer than stopping and starting?
