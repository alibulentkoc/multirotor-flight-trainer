# Lab 15. Hold against the wind

Level 2: Less help

Trainer address: https://alibulentkoc.github.io/multirotor-flight-trainer/

- You cannot break anything. This is a simulator. Crashing costs nothing.
- If anything goes wrong, press **R**. The drone returns to the start, good as new.
- There is no time limit. Only your best try counts.

**The keys**

```
W / S   up / down            Arrow Up / Down     forward / back
A / D   turn left / right    Arrow Left / Right  slide left / right
T  take off    L  land    R  start over    C  change view    P  flight plan
1  Position hold    2  Altitude hold    3  Stabilized
Space  motors on / off       Shift  full stick
```

Orange arms mark the front of the drone. Pressing **1** at any time gives you all the help back.

---

**Goal:** keep the drone in the box while wind pushes it.

**Start**
1. Open the trainer address.
2. Click once on the picture of the field.
3. In "Conditions and view", set **Wind at 10 m** to 4 m/s. Leave "Wind from" at 270 deg.
4. Under "Drills", click **Hover box**.

**Do**
1. Press **T** to take off. Tap **W** once or twice to center the drone in the box.
2. Press **2** for Altitude hold. The wind pushes the drone to your right.
3. Hold **Arrow Left** lightly to lean into the wind. Find the amount that stops the drift.
4. The wind comes in gusts. Adjust as it changes.
5. Stay in the green box until the counter reaches 15 s.
6. Look at the windsock on the field. It shows which way the wind blows.

**You are done when** you finish the drill once in Altitude hold with wind.

**If it blows away:** press **1**. The drone flies the wind for you. Go back to the box and press **2** again.

**Think about it:** the drone stayed still but leaned over. What was the lean doing?
