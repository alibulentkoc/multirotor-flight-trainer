# Lab 16. Through the gate

Level 2: Less help

Trainer address: https://alibulentkoc.github.io/multirotor-flight-trainer/

- You cannot break anything. This is a simulator. Crashing costs nothing.
- If anything goes wrong, press **R**. The drone returns to the start, good as new.
- There is no time limit. Only your best try counts.

**The keys**

```
W / S   up / down            Arrow Up / Down     forward / back
A / D   turn left / right    Arrow Left / Right  slide left / right
T  take off    L  land    R  start over    C  change view    P  flight plan
1  Position hold    2  Altitude hold    3  Stabilized
Space  motors on / off       Shift  full stick
```

Orange arms mark the front of the drone. Pressing **1** at any time gives you all the help back.

---

**Goal:** fly through a narrow gate with obstacle avoidance switched off.

**Start**
1. Open the trainer address.
2. Click once on the picture of the field.
3. Check that "Position hold" is selected at the top right.
4. In the Sensors section, **untick "Obstacle avoidance"**.
5. Find the gate. It is two white panels straight ahead, with a gap in the middle.

**Do**
1. Press **T** to take off. Tap **W** so "Altitude AGL" reads about 1.5 m.
2. Hold **Arrow Up** and fly slowly toward the gap.
3. Watch the range display in the Sensors section. It shows the distance to each panel.
4. Keep the left and right numbers about equal. That means you are centered.
5. Fly through the gap. Then hold **D** to turn around.
6. Fly back through the gap, nose-first, and return to the orange ring.
7. Press **L** to land.

**You are done when** you have gone through and come back without touching a panel.

**If you hit a panel:** press **R**. Go slower next time, and watch the two side numbers.

**Extra challenge:** do it again in Altitude hold. Press **2** after take off.

**Think about it:** the gap is 3.2 m wide. How did the side numbers help more than your eyes?
