# Lab 17. Measure the wind at height

Level 2: Less help

Trainer address: https://alibulentkoc.github.io/multirotor-flight-trainer/

- You cannot break anything. This is a simulator. Crashing costs nothing.
- If anything goes wrong, press **R**. The drone returns to the start, good as new.
- There is no time limit. Only your best try counts.

**The keys**

```
W / S   up / down            Arrow Up / Down     forward / back
A / D   turn left / right    Arrow Left / Right  slide left / right
T  take off    L  land    R  start over    C  change view    P  flight plan
1  Position hold    2  Altitude hold    3  Stabilized
Space  motors on / off       Shift  full stick
```

Orange arms mark the front of the drone. Pressing **1** at any time gives you all the help back.

---

**Goal:** find out how wind speed changes as you go higher.

**Start**
1. Open the trainer address.
2. Click once on the picture of the field.
3. Check that "Position hold" is selected at the top right.
4. In "Conditions and view", set **Wind at 10 m** to 8 m/s.

**Do**
1. Press **T** to take off.
2. Hold **W** until "Altitude AGL" reads about 2 m. Let go.
3. Watch "Wind here" in the Sensors section for ten seconds. It moves with the gusts. Write down the middle value.
4. Repeat at 5 m, 10 m, 20 m, and 40 m.
5. Hold **S** to come down to about 2 m. Then press **L** to land.
6. Draw a quick graph: height on the side, wind speed along the bottom.

| Height (m) | 2 | 5 | 10 | 20 | 40 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wind here (m/s) | | | | | |

**You are done when** your table is full.

**If the drone looks tiny at 40 m:** that is realistic. Press **C** for the chase view.

**Think about it:** a survey flies at 40 m. Is the wind up there stronger or weaker than what you feel on the ground?
