# Lab 20. Inspect the barn

Level 2: Less help

Trainer address: https://alibulentkoc.github.io/multirotor-flight-trainer/

- You cannot break anything. This is a simulator. Crashing costs nothing.
- If anything goes wrong, press **R**. The drone returns to the start, good as new.
- There is no time limit. Only your best try counts.

**The keys**

```
W / S   up / down            Arrow Up / Down     forward / back
A / D   turn left / right    Arrow Left / Right  slide left / right
T  take off    L  land    R  start over    C  change view    P  flight plan
1  Position hold    2  Altitude hold    3  Stabilized
Space  motors on / off       Shift  full stick
```

Orange arms mark the front of the drone. Pressing **1** at any time gives you all the help back.

---

**Goal:** build a waypoint route around the barn, with a photo and a hover, and fly it.

**Start**
1. Open the trainer address.
2. Click once on the picture of the field.
3. Press **P**. The flight plan window opens.
4. Click **Waypoint route** at the top of the window.

**Do**
1. Set "Altitude above ground" to 8 **before** you click the map. New waypoints use this height.
2. Set "Speed" to 3.
3. On the map, find the barn. It is the largest grey block, to the left of the **H**.
4. Click four points around the barn, one near each corner. Numbered circles appear.
5. In the table under the map, set waypoint 2 to **Take a photo**.
6. Set waypoint 3 to **Hover 5 s**.
7. Click **Fly this plan**. Press **P** to close the window, then **C** for the chase view.
8. Watch it fly the route, pause, and return home to land.

**You are done when** the drone has flown around the barn and landed.

**Important:** the barn is about 5 m tall. On a plan, the drone does not dodge obstacles. Your chosen height is the safety margin. Keep it at 8 m or more.

**If a waypoint is in the wrong place:** open the window with **P** and drag the circle.

**Think about it:** why does a planned flight need a height margin above everything on its path?
