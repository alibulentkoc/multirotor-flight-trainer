Lab 21. Nose-in, by hand

Level 3: On your own
  • You cannot break anything. This is a simulator. Crashing costs nothing.
  • If anything goes wrong, press R. The drone returns to the start, good as new.
  • There is no time limit. Only your best try counts.
Show the keys
W / S   up / down            Arrow Up / Down     forward / back
A / D   turn left / right    Arrow Left / Right  slide left / right
T  take off    L  land    R  start over    C  change view    P  flight plan
1  Position hold    2  Altitude hold    3  Stabilized
Space  motors on / off       Shift  full stick

Orange arms mark the front of the drone. Pressing 1 at any time gives you all the help back.

Goal: hover in the box with the nose toward you, with no position or height help.

Start

  1. Click Open the trainer at the top of this page. Use Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.
  2. Click once on the picture of the field.
  3. In the Sensors section, check that "Landing assist" is ticked. It is ticked by default.
  4. Under "Drills", click Nose-in hover.

Do

  1. Press T to take off. Tap W once or twice to center the drone in the box.
  2. Hold D until the orange arms point toward you. The heading reads about 180.
  3. Press 3 for Stabilized.
  4. Left and right are now swapped on your screen. So are forward and back. Think from the drone's seat, not yours.
  5. Use very short taps. Correct one direction at a time.
  6. Stay in the green box until the counter reaches 15 s.

You are done when you finish the drill once. Any score is fine.

If it gets away from you: press 1. The drone stops and holds. Breathe, then press 3 again.

Think about it: what trick helped you pick the correct key when the nose faced you?
