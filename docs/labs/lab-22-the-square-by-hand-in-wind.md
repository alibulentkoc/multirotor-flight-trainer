# Lab 22. The square, by hand, in wind

Level 3: On your own

Trainer address: https://alibulentkoc.github.io/multirotor-flight-trainer/

- You cannot break anything. This is a simulator. Crashing costs nothing.
- If anything goes wrong, press **R**. The drone returns to the start, good as new.
- There is no time limit. Only your best try counts.

**The keys**

```
W / S   up / down            Arrow Up / Down     forward / back
A / D   turn left / right    Arrow Left / Right  slide left / right
T  take off    L  land    R  start over    C  change view    P  flight plan
1  Position hold    2  Altitude hold    3  Stabilized
Space  motors on / off       Shift  full stick
```

Orange arms mark the front of the drone. Pressing **1** at any time gives you all the help back.

---

**Goal:** fly the four markers in Stabilized mode with a light wind.

**Start**
1. Open the trainer address.
2. Click once on the picture of the field.
3. In "Conditions and view", set **Wind at 10 m** to 3 m/s. Leave "Wind from" at 270 deg.
4. Under "Drills", click **Square circuit**. Four round markers appear.

**Do**
1. Press **T** to take off. Tap **W** once or twice to match the height of the markers.
2. Press **3** for Stabilized.
3. Fly to the orange marker. Brake before you reach it, or you will sail past.
4. The wind pushes you to the right the whole time. Lean against it.
5. Watch "Altitude AGL". Add a tap of **W** each time you tilt, because tilting makes it sink.
6. Keep the nose pointing away from you. Do not use **A** or **D**.
7. After the fourth marker, your score appears.

**You are done when** you complete the square with a score of 40 or more.

**If you keep overshooting:** fly slower. Let go early and brake with the opposite key.

**Think about it:** which legs were hardest, the ones along the wind or the ones across it?
