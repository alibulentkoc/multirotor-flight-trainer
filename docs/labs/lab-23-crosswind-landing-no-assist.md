# Lab 23. Crosswind landing, no assist

Level 3: On your own

Trainer address: https://alibulentkoc.github.io/multirotor-flight-trainer/

- You cannot break anything. This is a simulator. Crashing costs nothing.
- If anything goes wrong, press **R**. The drone returns to the start, good as new.
- There is no time limit. Only your best try counts.

**The keys**

```
W / S   up / down            Arrow Up / Down     forward / back
A / D   turn left / right    Arrow Left / Right  slide left / right
T  take off    L  land    R  start over    C  change view    P  flight plan
1  Position hold    2  Altitude hold    3  Stabilized
Space  motors on / off       Shift  full stick
```

Orange arms mark the front of the drone. Pressing **1** at any time gives you all the help back.

---

**Goal:** land on the target by hand while the wind pushes you sideways.

**Start**
1. Open the trainer address.
2. Click once on the picture of the field.
3. In the Sensors section, **untick "Landing assist"**.
4. In "Conditions and view", set **Wind at 10 m** to 4 m/s. Leave "Wind from" at 270 deg.
5. Under "Drills", click **Precision landing**. A round target appears ahead and to the right.

**Do**
1. Press **T** to take off. Fly to the target with the arrow keys.
2. Put the crosshair of the "Down camera" picture on the center of the target.
3. Press **2** for Altitude hold. The wind starts to push you off the target.
4. Hold the drone over the target by leaning into the wind.
5. Tap **S** to come down. Keep "Climb" between -0.5 and -1.0 m/s.
6. The wind gets weaker near the ground. Ease off your lean as you descend.
7. Touch down level and without sliding. Press **Space** to switch the motors off.

**You are done when** you land within 1.0 m of the center, without a crash.

**If you crash for sliding sideways:** you were still leaning at touchdown. Level out in the last half meter.

**Think about it:** why did you need less lean close to the ground?
