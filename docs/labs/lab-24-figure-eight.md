# Lab 24. Figure eight

Level 3: On your own

Trainer address: https://alibulentkoc.github.io/multirotor-flight-trainer/

- You cannot break anything. This is a simulator. Crashing costs nothing.
- If anything goes wrong, press **R**. The drone returns to the start, good as new.
- There is no time limit. Only your best try counts.

**The keys**

```
W / S   up / down            Arrow Up / Down     forward / back
A / D   turn left / right    Arrow Left / Right  slide left / right
T  take off    L  land    R  start over    C  change view    P  flight plan
1  Position hold    2  Altitude hold    3  Stabilized
Space  motors on / off       Shift  full stick
```

Orange arms mark the front of the drone. Pressing **1** at any time gives you all the help back.

---

**Goal:** fly a smooth figure eight around the tree and the hay bales, nose-first.

**Start**
1. Open the trainer address.
2. Click once on the picture of the field.
3. Find the tree, ahead and to the left. Find the hay bales, to your right.

**Do**
1. Press **T** to take off. Hold **W** until "Altitude AGL" reads about 4 m.
2. Press **2** for Altitude hold.
3. Hold **Arrow Up** and steer with **A** and **D**, like a car.
4. Pass between the two objects. Go around the tree to the left.
5. Come back between them. Go around the bales to the right.
6. That is one figure eight. Fly three in a row without stopping.
7. Fly back to the orange ring. Press **1**, then **L** to land.

**You are done when** you have flown three figure eights without touching anything.

**Good to know:** in Altitude hold, obstacle avoidance only warns you. You must steer clear yourself.

**If you lose track of the nose:** stop, find the orange arms, then continue.

**Think about it:** at which point of the eight was the drone flying toward you? What did you do differently there?
