# Lab 25. The gate, by hand

Level 3: On your own

Trainer address: https://alibulentkoc.github.io/multirotor-flight-trainer/

- You cannot break anything. This is a simulator. Crashing costs nothing.
- If anything goes wrong, press **R**. The drone returns to the start, good as new.
- There is no time limit. Only your best try counts.

**The keys**

```
W / S   up / down            Arrow Up / Down     forward / back
A / D   turn left / right    Arrow Left / Right  slide left / right
T  take off    L  land    R  start over    C  change view    P  flight plan
1  Position hold    2  Altitude hold    3  Stabilized
Space  motors on / off       Shift  full stick
```

Orange arms mark the front of the drone. Pressing **1** at any time gives you all the help back.

---

**Goal:** fly through the gate and back in Stabilized mode, watching from where you stand.

**Start**
1. Open the trainer address.
2. Click once on the picture of the field.
3. In the Sensors section, **untick "Obstacle avoidance"**.
4. Check that the view at the top reads "View: pilot". Do not change it in this lab.
5. Find the gate. It is two white panels straight ahead, with a gap in the middle.

**Do**
1. Press **T** to take off. Tap **W** so "Altitude AGL" reads about 1.5 m.
2. Press **3** for Stabilized.
3. Fly slowly toward the gap. Hold your height with taps of **W** and **S**.
4. Glance at the range display. Keep the left and right numbers about equal.
5. Pass through. Brake. Hold **D** to turn around.
6. Fly back through the gap, nose-first.
7. Return to the orange ring and land by hand. Press **Space** to switch the motors off.

**You are done when** you have gone through and back once without a crash.

**If this feels too hard:** do it first in Altitude hold (press **2**). Then come back to Stabilized.

**Think about it:** the gate is 13 m away. What made it hard to judge your distance to it?
