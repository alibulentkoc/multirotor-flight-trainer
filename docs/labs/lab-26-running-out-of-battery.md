# Lab 26. Running out of battery

Level 3: On your own

Trainer address: https://alibulentkoc.github.io/multirotor-flight-trainer/

- You cannot break anything. This is a simulator. Crashing costs nothing.
- If anything goes wrong, press **R**. The drone returns to the start, good as new.
- There is no time limit. Only your best try counts.

**The keys**

```
W / S   up / down            Arrow Up / Down     forward / back
A / D   turn left / right    Arrow Left / Right  slide left / right
T  take off    L  land    R  start over    C  change view    P  flight plan
1  Position hold    2  Altitude hold    3  Stabilized
Space  motors on / off       Shift  full stick
```

Orange arms mark the front of the drone. Pressing **1** at any time gives you all the help back.

---

**Goal:** see what happens when a plan is too big for the battery, then fix the plan.

**Start**
1. Open the trainer address.
2. Click once on the picture of the field.
3. Press **P**. The flight plan window opens.
4. Check that **Survey area** is selected.

**Do**
1. Set "Battery endurance" to 10. Set "Altitude above ground" to 30.
2. Click **Zoom out** once. Click four corners of a large square that fills most of the map above the **H**.
3. A red warning says the plan needs more than 80 percent of the battery. If it does not appear, drag the corners outward.
4. Fly it anyway. Set **8x time** and click **Fly this plan**.
5. Watch "Battery". At 20 percent the drone gives up the plan and flies home. Note how much of the field has green dots.
6. Now fix the plan. Change the altitude, the speed, or the area until the warning goes away.
7. Fly the fixed plan. It should finish with battery to spare.

**You are done when** one flight has ended early and one has finished the whole plan.

**Think about it:** you had three ways to fix the plan. What did each one cost you?
