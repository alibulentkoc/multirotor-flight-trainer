# Lab 30. Checkride

Level 3: On your own

Trainer address: https://alibulentkoc.github.io/multirotor-flight-trainer/

- You cannot break anything. This is a simulator. Crashing costs nothing.
- If anything goes wrong, press **R**. The drone returns to the start, good as new.
- There is no time limit. Only your best try counts.

**The keys**

```
W / S   up / down            Arrow Up / Down     forward / back
A / D   turn left / right    Arrow Left / Right  slide left / right
T  take off    L  land    R  start over    C  change view    P  flight plan
1  Position hold    2  Altitude hold    3  Stabilized
Space  motors on / off       Shift  full stick
```

Orange arms mark the front of the drone. Pressing **1** at any time gives you all the help back.

---

**Goal:** fly a complete manual flight, from take off to landing, with no help and no reset.

**Start**
1. Open the trainer address.
2. Click once on the picture of the field.
3. In the Sensors section, **untick "Landing assist"** and **untick "Obstacle avoidance"**.
4. In "Conditions and view", set **Wind at 10 m** to 3 m/s.
5. Check that the view at the top reads "View: pilot".
6. Press **3** for Stabilized.

**Do, in one flight**
1. Press **Space** to start the motors. Hold **W** to lift off. Climb to about 3 m and hold a steady hover for ten seconds.
2. Fly nose-first to the tree and go once around it.
3. Descend to about 1.5 m. Fly through the gate and back.
4. Turn the nose toward you. Fly back to the orange ring, nose-in.
5. Hover over the ring for five seconds.
6. Land by hand on the ring, at less than 1 m/s. Press **Space** to switch the motors off.

**You pass when** you complete all six steps in one flight without a crash and without pressing **1** or **R**.

**This is hard.** Most people need many tries. Each step is a lab you have already done. If one step keeps failing, go back to that lab, then return.

**Think about it:** which step needed the most attention? What would you practice before flying a real drone?
